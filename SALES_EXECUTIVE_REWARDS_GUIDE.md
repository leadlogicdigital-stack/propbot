## 💰 Coin System Explained

### How You Earn Coins

```
BASE EARNING:
├─ 1 Coin: Per approved property submission
│
BONUS COINS:
├─ +5 Coins: After 5 consecutive approved submissions (streak)
├─ +10 Coins: After 10 consecutive approved submissions
├─ +15 Coins: After 20 submissions in a calendar month
└─ Streak resets if submission is rejected
```

### Example Monthly Earnings

```
Month: March 2026

Week 1:
├─ Submit 5 properties
├─ All 5 approved → 5 coins + 5 bonus (5-streak) = 10 coins ✓

Week 2:
├─ Submit 6 more properties

Week 3:
├─ Submit 5 properties

├─ Submit 4 more properties

MONTH TOTAL: 10 + 6 + 4 + 19 = 39 coins!
```

## 📞 Support & FAQ

### Q: How often are coins awarded?
**A:** Immediately when your submission is approved by admin.

### Q: Can I lose coins if a submission is rejected?
**A:** No! Coins are only awarded for approved submissions. Rejections don't affect your balance.

### Q: What happens if my streak breaks?
**A:** Your consecutive submission count resets to 0, but your total earned coins remain. Keep submitting to build a new streak!

### Q: Can I transfer coins to other executives?
**A:** No, coins are personal rewards. But team members can share leads after redemption.

### Q: What if a redeemed lead doesn't convert?
**A:** No refund needed—leads are one-time access. Use your outreach skills to build relationships.

### Q: How do I access redeemed leads?
**A:** Download them from "My Redeemed Leads" section as CSV, import to CRM, or get direct contact info.

### Q: Can I save coins for later?
**A:** Yes! Coins don't expire. Save up for premium packages if you prefer bulk leads.

### Q: Is there a maximum coin balance?
**A:** No limit! Some top performers have 100+ coins saved.
